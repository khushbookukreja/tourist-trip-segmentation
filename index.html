Tourist trip segmentation

Each dot is one trip described by 11 attributes. PCA and t-SNE squash those attributes into two dimensions so trips with the same purpose land near each other. Data is synthetic and generated in your browser.

Hover a dot to see the same trip in both plots. Click a purpose to hide or show it.

Colour runs from low (blue) to high (red) for the chosen attribute, clipped at two standard deviations. Use it to see what each region of the map means.

What drives each PCA axis

Bars show the five attributes with the largest weight on each axis. Opposite colours pull in opposite directions, and the overall sign of an axis is arbitrary.
